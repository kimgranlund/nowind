Surfaces

Materials

Massimo's materials, one per region: material-flat, -physical, -frosted, -glass and -outline. Each part names its relief (relief-ground, relief-raised, relief-raised-solid, relief-field); the material decides how it is lit, lined or made of glass. The dot grid behind (bg-dots) shows how much each material blurs; glass adds lens, which bends the dots along its rim where the browser can refract.

Frequently asked

Do I need a build step?

No. Link one stylesheet; every class already exists.

Does it work in a CMS?

Yes. Any class works in any HTML, including what a CMS writes.

How do I change the brand color?

Point accent-1 at another color family in its palette file and rebuild the bundle.
