Release notes

What changed in each release of Nowind, newest first. Pin a release in the CDN link and your site changes only when you change the version.

Pin a release

0.10.0

2026-11-25 Latest
  • Pickers and menus open below

    The select's picker and the anchor-* popovers set position-try-order: normal, so they open below their trigger and flip only when they don't fit; the browser's default chose whichever side had more room, so a field low on the screen opened its list above.

  • Geometry audit

    python3 scripts/templates.py --geometry-report checks each example part's height, padding, gap and corner against the geometry contract, and ?audit=geometry on any page measures the rendered examples: corners that should nest, uneven insets at a container's end, text against a painted edge, side-by-side controls of different heights, squares and circles that aren't, and clipped content. Both report nothing on the showcase and all 26 component pages at 375, 716 and 1280px and at 12 and 18px roots. The examples lint enforces the contract (self-test: seventeen breaks), and the Reference page has a "Where each length goes" table.

  • The reset zeroes button padding,

    As Tailwind's preflight does: icon items in segmented controls and toolbars (h-full aspect-square) had kept the browser's 1px 6px and come out 35 × 32px instead of square.

  • Geometry contract

    A table in the geometry manifest (geometryContract) gives each kind of part its height, padding, gap and corner. Following it, one-line alerts space icon and text gap-gutter like toasts, and the tooltip pads py-2xs px-xs.

  • Callouts in primitives

    The docs' callouts and the callout sample are p-md border-s-4 border-{palette}-7 bg-{palette}-3 with fg-{palette}-11 icons, text-sm font-medium fg-{palette}-12 labels and text-base text, in place of role classes; the block sample's heading is text-heading-md, as the pages use.

  • One corner for alerts

    One-line alerts are rounded-md, like titled alerts and toasts.

  • Pressed segments are white in both modes, flat

    pressed:bg-white pressed:fg-black with no shadow. New bg-white, bg-black, fg-white and fg-black (the neutral contrast ends, the same in light and dark), with state variants.

  • Unread dot

    A block span, so its padding draws a circle; inline, it took the line's height and drew a tall pill.

  • Tag chips fixed

    A chip is px-nested with no fixed height, so its size-nested remove button sets the height and px-nested drops to zero at that trailing control: the circle meets the pill on three sides; the badge pass had stacked px-nested on the chip's own padding. The examples lint now catches a shorthand padding (p-, px-, py-) set alongside a one-side padding.

  • Soft buttons without a border

    The accent soft button is bg-accent-1-3 with a transparent border, matching the secondary button.

  • No clipped cards when rows stack

    Items that share a row use md:flex-1, not flex-1, so below md, where the row becomes a column, they keep their content height; the media card had shrunk to zero basis and overflow-hidden cut off its text.

  • Menus anchor to their trigger

    The select's picker and every anchor-* popover start flush with the trigger's edge and are at least as wide as it (anchor-size(width)), with the 3xs gap only on the side facing it; before, the gap applied on every side and the picker floated inset and narrower than its field.

  • Lighter field borders

    Text fields, selects and textareas keep their white face and a visible border, lighter now: border-neutral-5 at rest, hover:border-neutral-7, accent on focus (was 6 and 8); the reset gives a bare field --border-weak.

  • Nested parts, one geometry

    Badges, the actions inside alerts and toasts, and toolbar items are all the small control: h-nested with px-nested (which steps in at an icon), and the alert's secondary action is a soft tint (bg-danger-a3) instead of bare text. A count badge and the stepper's numbers are 1:1 circles (size-nested, text-xs), with a count row in the size contract. The toolbar and segmented containers take the secondary buttons' light tint (bg-neutral-a2).

  • Badges share one geometry

    Every badge is h-nested, rounded-full and bordered (transparent on solid fills), padded px-2xs, or ps-3xs pe-2xs with a leading icon; a count is min-w-nested so it stays round. Outlined and solid badges were different heights, and the Beta tag a different shape.

  • Secondary buttons, softer

    A light translucent neutral tint (bg-neutral-a2) with no visible border (border border-transparent, so the height is unchanged) in place of a grey fill and a mid-grey border; the state layer gives hover and press. The file input's button matches.

  • Site map links work on the showcase

    The showcase's demo script, which moves aria-current on click, caught every link in a role="list" list, the site map's included, and cancelled the navigation. It now acts only on placeholder links (href="#") inside the page's main content.

  • Site map links

    Each link is as wide as its label with the nested corner (w-fit rounded-xs), so a hover is a small wash rather than a full-column pill, and the current page is marked with an accent tint instead of the same grey as a hover.

0.9.0

2026-11-25
  • Docs chrome on the size contract

    The Use and Avoid marks are icon beside body text (they were icon-sm), and a code caption sets its language label at the filename's size.

  • Leaner variants (60.4 → 47.2 KiB gzip for the linked file)

    Hover and state variants cover the colour roles plus the raw steps a control moves between (surfaces 1–5, solids 9–10, text 11–12 and contrast, borders 6–8) in neutral, accent-1 and danger; other steps use state. Breakpoints cover display, grid, flex, alignment, widths, inline padding and gaps (no position, heights or overflow). Container queries are @sm:, @md:, @lg: over the classes that change a component's layout. Nothing the site uses was removed.

  • Stronger hovers

    The state layer's opacities are 12% hover, 14% focus, 16% selected and 20% pressed (from 8, 10, 12 and 16), and the site map's links take the state layer with a nested-control shape.

  • Section titles

    The site's section headings (showcase, utilities and every docs page) are text-heading-md (the text-xl step) instead of text-subtitle (the base size), so they stand above the body text under them.

  • Tailwind's scales, then Nowind's systems

    Every type step is Tailwind v4's fraction of the base (xs 0.75 … 6xl 3.75) with Tailwind's line heights, so at a 16px base the sizes are Tailwind's exactly; Utopia's fluid base, the root size, tiers and --F then act on that base. The Utopia ratio options (--min-ratio, --max-ratio) are gone. Space, breakpoints and container widths already matched Tailwind's. Large steps grow in proportion to the base now, rather than faster than it on wide screens.

  • A gentler small step

    text-sm is 0.875 × base and text-xs 0.75 × base (14 and 12px at 16, Tailwind's ratios) at every width, instead of one and two Utopia ratio steps down (13.3 and 11px), which made supporting text drop too far below body text. The steps above base keep Utopia's ratio; icon-sm follows text-sm (0.875 F).

  • Select picker in Phosphor, nested

    The select's caret, the caret class and the selected option's checkmark are Phosphor's caret-down and check (bold), inlined as mask tokens (--glyph-caret, --glyph-check), so nothing extra loads. The picker is rounded-md padded by the inset and its options are a control tall, rounded-sm, so their corners nest concentrically.

  • Breadcrumbs at the navigation size

    text-base, like the tabs and links around them; the size contract gains a navigation row.

  • Sizes examples

    The Button, Icon button, Segmented control and Text field pages show small, default and large. A size is a region with its own base: scope text-sm or scope text-lg around the component, so height, padding, gaps, icons and corners all recompute and still nest (25, 37 and 51px at a 16px root), and the component's text inherits the scope's size. Both type audits treat a scope as relative.

  • Softer curvature

    --radius-offset is -4px (Affine uses -6px), so every radius step gains 2px: at 16px the control corner is 12px and the nested step 5px, where 3px had read as sharp on keycaps and nested items. Nesting stays exact. Keycaps set text-sm.

  • README rewritten on Prewind's structure

    What Nowind is for (a runtime, no-dependency foundation for agents and generated UI, with sensible defaults and a retunable token layer), what it derives from (Prewind, Tailwind's class system, Radix Colors, Utopia) and what it adds (one-input geometry, root-scaled fluid sizing, tiers); a Nowind, Prewind and Tailwind comparison, a reference and a variants table. Stale examples (a padded field box, semantic classes) replaced with the current patterns.

  • Type audit

    A size contract in the type-roles manifest (sizeContract) says which size each kind of text takes; python3 scripts/templates.py --type-report checks every example element against it, and ?audit=type on any page checks the rendered examples for rows that mix sizes, titles smaller than their body, and icons a step off their text. The sweep brought every example onto the contract: 12 body paragraphs, 8 pricing features (with their ✓ at icon), 6 activity and notification lines (inline names and code now take the line's size) and the drop zone title move to text-base; the spinner beside small text is icon-sm and the selected-option check icon. Both passes now report nothing, and the examples lint enforces the contract (self-test: sixteen breaks); the Type and space page has a "Where each size goes" table.

  • `icon-sm` is the small text step

    --icon-sm is F ÷ 1.2 (13.3px at 16) instead of F, so a small icon matches the text-sm label beside it in chips, badges and toolbars, where it had been larger than the text; carets get finer too. Marks keep their size (--mark-sm is F).

  • Dialog type

    The title is text-lg, a step above the controls, and the body text-base, the controls' size (it had been text-base and text-sm, so the body read smaller than the buttons and the title no larger); footer buttons sit gap-xs apart.

  • Actions in toasts and alerts nest

    Retry, Undo, View logs and Dismiss take rounded-inner, the surface's radius less its padding, instead of rounded-xs, the radius for an item one inset inside a control, which read as square at a toast's padding. The titled alert pads p-xs, as the toast does (at p-sm the inner radius came to 1.5px).

  • Dialog example

    The close button is a fixed size-nested shrink-0 square again (an earlier toolbar change had made it h-full aspect-square, so it grew with a wrapped title), and the dialog example has its own full row (capped at max-w-form), so its title and its Cancel and Delete buttons no longer wrap; the buttons sit gap-xs apart.

  • Search field

    The showcase's search box is the standard field box (h-control px-control), so the ⌘K keycap sits one inset from the top, bottom and end and its rounded-xs nests in the field's corner. It had been padded (py-xs ps-xs pe-sm), taller than a control with an uneven inset. The examples lint now rejects a field box sized by padding (self-test: fifteen breaks).

  • Website field

    The showcase's https:// prefix is the standard prefix slot (plain fg-neutral-11 text in the field box) instead of a grey add-on band that didn't fill the field's height.

  • Tables at the control size

    Example table cells and headers set text-base (inherited) instead of text-sm, and take the control geometry (h-control px-control), so a row is a control's height and its text starts where a button's would.

  • Sized headings read as text

    The reset's heading roles (face, weight, tracking) now apply only to an h1–h6 without a text-{size} class. A card title written <h3 class="text-base font-semibold"> had kept the heading face (Inter Tight, tight tracking) and looked larger than the same size elsewhere, such as a list item's name; now it matches. Role classes (text-title, text-heading-md, …) still set their own face, and font-heading adds it back by hand.

  • Toolbar on the segmented geometry

    h-control rounded-sm border-2 border-transparent bg-neutral-3 with touching h-full rounded-inner items and an inset divider, so the toolbar, the alignment group and the segmented control share one inset, height and corner.

  • Display figures

    Prices and metrics set in font-display (Inter Tight, new) at font-extrabold (800, new) with tracking-tight; the price caption stays on one line.

  • Sidebar nav on the segmented geometry

    rounded-sm border-2 border-transparent with touching rounded-inner items, as the segmented control.

  • One keycap

    Every <kbd> is h-nested min-w-nested px-2xs rounded-xs border, so a single key is square and a chord sits gap-3xs apart.

  • Nested curvature

    The one-line toast's Undo sits in the toast's end corner, so it takes rounded-inner and follows the toast's radius less its padding.

  • Round markers are circles

    Stepper numbers, avatars and initials are size-nested or size-control with shrink-0; padded, they took their width from the text and came out oval.

  • No conflicting classes in the examples

    The lint now rejects two classes that set the same property on one element (the colour picker had p-3xs and p-0; the read-only and disabled fields stacked a second background, colour and cursor on the field's own). Self-test: fourteen breaks.

  • Avatars

    Each is size-control shrink-0, one control height square whatever the initials (they were padded, so they varied in width and shrank in a tight row); the showcase group keeps its avatars together and wraps the caption below them instead of one word per line.

  • Command palette geometry

    The palette is rounded-md p-inset border holding rounded-sm items, the menu's nesting; the search row is an h-control px-control row at the same inset, so its icon lines up with every item's icon; group labels sit on the item text edge (px-control). esc and every shortcut are one keycap (h-nested px-2xs rounded-xs border), which px-control places at the icon padding from the end.

  • Toasts, rebuilt

    Fixed slots (icon, a text column of title, detail and actions, dismiss) with items-start, so the icon and dismiss stay on the title's line however the text wraps and every toast in a stack lays out the same; even p-xs padding, a light action button on the dark face, a readable dismiss (fg-neutral-8) whose corner nests (rounded-inner). A one-line toast keeps its action inline. The toast page shows both.

  • Tag chips

    The remove button sits the same distance (3xs) from the chip's top, bottom and end edges, with gap-3xs before it.

  • Timeline

    The rail runs unbroken from one marker to the next (the item gap moved from the row to the text), at 2px.

  • Control gaps

    Buttons, fields, selects, menu items and toolbar items space their icon and label by the control gap, gap-gutter (one margin, 7px at 16) or gap-gutter-half when nested, in place of gap-xs (12px); both count as regular classes now. px-control and px-nested also drop to the icon padding at a trailing icon (a field's warning icon had the text padding). The examples lint enforces the gap (self-test: twelve breaks).

  • Badges

    Icon and label sit gap-3xs apart (they were gap-2xs, which with the icon box's own side space read as a wide gap).

  • Segmented controls, tight

    The box is h-control rounded-sm border-2 border-transparent (a 2px inset, its background showing through the border) and the segments are h-full rounded-inner (aspect-square for icons), so they fill a control's height and their corners follow the box exactly.

  • Display settings panel

    The header bar's tier, size and light/dark buttons are now one settings button that opens a panel: theme (light, dark, system), base size (12 to 18px) and tier, each a segmented control. The panel is scope text-chrome, a fixed 14px (--text-chrome), so its controls stay 30px whatever the root size or tier. New: text-chrome and anchor-bottom-end.

  • Small controls live in an inset container

    Filter, Sort and Close now sit in a toolbar (p-inset rounded-sm border, a control's height) rather than loose beside full-size buttons. The text-alignment group is an icon segmented control (size-nested squares in an inset box) instead of three joined 38px squares with square outer corners. Segments touch (gap-0) and the pressed one lifts with the new pressed:shadow-xs; shadow-xs, shadow-sm and shadow-none now have selected:, current:, pressed:, open: and has-checked: variants. The Icon button page gains a toolbar example and the Segmented control page an icon example. The examples lint rejects a small control beside full-size ones (self-test: eleven breaks), and scripts/check.mjs now fails when the lint itself crashes instead of passing silently.

  • Tier toggle on the site

    A button in the header bar cycles Auto (each region's own tier), product, content and brand, and remembers the choice. It swaps the tier on the page and every example frame; the tiers demo keeps its three.

  • Tiers no longer stack

    Each tier sets its font size to the base plus its own step (var(--text-base)) instead of adding to the inherited size. A tier-product frame inside a tier-content page had come out one content step too large (a 31px button where 23px was meant).

  • The fluid scale follows the root size

    The vw part of every Utopia clamp is now --vw, 1vw times the root size over 16px (CSS typed arithmetic, calc(1vw * (1rem / 16px))), so type, space, radii and controls scale with the root font size at any width. A 12px root gives 0.75 of every 16px size; controls step 4px per root pixel (23, 27, 31, 35, 39, 43, 47 at 12 to 18 on an 873px window). Browsers without typed arithmetic keep plain 1vw.

  • Controls back on the control geometry

    The control lengths (h-control, px-control, size-control, min-w-control and the -nested forms) count as regular classes, like the icon sizes, and every button, field, select, menu item and tab in the examples uses them again. They had been padded with py-xs px-sm, about 40px tall at any base size; now they are Affine's 4n − 26 (22px at 12, 38px at 16). The examples lint rejects a control sized by padding, and its square-icon-button check now actually runs on buttons (self-test: ten breaks). The showcase runs at tier-product, as the component pages' example frames do, so its controls are product-sized rather than built from the reading tier's base + 2px.

  • Alerts and toasts, tighter and sized on purpose

    A one-line alert takes a field's geometry (py-xs ps-xs pe-sm rounded-sm) and the base icon; a titled alert pads p-sm, puts icon-lg on the title row and runs the detail and actions full width below. The alert page shows both. Toasts pad py-2xs ps-sm pe-2xs with text-base messages, and their actions use state.

  • State layer

    state washes the text colour over an element, under its content, at --state-hover (0.08), --state-focus (0.1), --state-selected (0.12: selected, pressed, expanded, current or a checked child) and --state-active (0.16), and shows nothing when disabled. One class gives any element in any palette its states, a transparent one included, in place of a hover: and active: class per step. The examples use it: 75 elements trade their hover:/active: bg variants for state; 22 whose selected, pressed or current state changes the surface (a solid fill, a lighter segment) keep theirs. The step variants stay for authors, and the examples lint rejects a hover tint where state would do (self-test: eight breaks).

  • Icon edges

    A control or field that starts or ends with an icon pads that edge at its block padding (ps-xs pe-sm, or px-xs with icons at both ends), as px-control does, so the icon sits in a square cell; 60 examples had picked up the wider text padding. The examples lint checks it (self-test: nine breaks).

  • Base size toggle on the site

    A button beside light and dark cycles the root font size through 12, 14, 16 and 18px and remembers it. Type, space, radii and control heights all follow, since they are built on rem and --F; the breakpoints and container widths stay in px.

  • Container queries

    Put @container on a wrapper and its descendants take @sm: to @2xl: (384 to 672px, the --container-* tokens) on the same layout classes as the breakpoints, so a component follows its own width. The media card uses it: the media moves beside the text once the card is 448px wide. The media card also loses a padding that p-0 could not override.

  • Logical properties (Tailwind v4 names)

    ps-/pe-, ms-/me- (and -ms-/-me-, ms-auto/me-auto), start-0/end-0/-full, border-s/border-e, rounded-s-*/rounded-e-*. The examples and the site use them in place of left and right, so they mirror in right-to-left languages, and the examples lint rejects the physical forms.

  • Breaking, one name per radius

    rounded, rounded-control and rounded-nested are removed; use rounded-sm (the control radius) and rounded-xs (one margin less).

  • Leaner examples

    232 interactive elements drop transition-colors/transition, which the reset already gives them; only elements that aren't interactive name a transition.

  • Borders start in the reset

    As in Tailwind's preflight, every element starts with a zero-width solid border, so border, border-t, border-b-2 and the rest set only a width, and border-dashed or border-dotted combine with any of them. Text fields, textareas and selects keep a default border and hr keeps its line. Tabs in the examples are underlined only (they had a stray full border).

  • Context kept current

    AGENTS.md has a rule that every change updates its manifest, entry files, docs and changelog in the same commit, and scripts/check.mjs now fails when AGENTS.md stops naming a repo file or names a path that doesn't exist (self-test: seven breaks). The class manifest's radius, icon, select, spacing and layout descriptions are brought up to date.

  • Icon classes are primitives

    icon, icon-sm, icon-md and icon-lg are lengths from the base size, so they count as regular classes and the examples use them: <i class="icon ph ph-gear"> in place of five layout classes and a type size (which made icons about 18% too big). Carets are icon icon-sm. The examples lint requires every Phosphor icon to sit in an icon box.

  • Square icon buttons

    Every icon-only button in the examples pads the same on all sides (p-xs, or p-3xs when small), so it is square; the examples lint rejects uneven padding on one.

  • Docs pages

    Previous and next sit side by side at every width; table cells pad less on a phone; a class name in a table breaks only at a hyphen or colon, never mid-word.

  • Radii nest, after Affine UI

    One unit runs the radius scale: rounded-xs (control − margin), rounded-sm (control), new rounded-md (control + margin), rounded-lg (+2 margins) and rounded-xl (+3). New p-inset (margin − border) pads between levels, so rounded-md p-inset border holds rounded-sm items concentrically. New rounded-inner gives any item the parent's radius minus its padding and border. Breaking: rounded-lg was the card radius and is now the dialog's; cards are rounded-md, dialogs rounded-lg.

  • Docs for the system

    New Colour, Type and space, States and variants, and Materials pages, their tables read from the manifests (state-variant counts from nowind.css). The docs home's figures come from the manifests too. The README covers the lens, backdrops, fields, native controls, default transitions and the new files.

  • Single-line controls never wrap

    The reset keeps buttons, selects and inputs on one line. In select-base, a <button> holding <selectedcontent> lets a long value end in an ellipsis before the caret; select-base no longer zeroes its block padding. Toasts move their actions to a second line instead of squeezing the message. New basis-0.

  • Carets are always the small icon size

    caret, the select's picker icon and every caret in the examples (checked by the examples lint).

  • Showcase form to the field standard

    Every field is a slot box with a leading icon for the kind of value, a bare input, focus-within: and has-invalid: on the box, units in a suffix, a themed select, and a counted bio. New has-checked: state variant lets a choice card follow its checked radio. select-base puts its caret at the end of the field.

  • Manifests

    components.manifest.json lists every component as data (page, group, summary, use, avoid, classes). nowind.manifest.json counts state and group variants and describes rotation, the open-popover group and the contrast classes; the materials manifest describes the lens.

  • Transitions by default

    The reset gives every interactive element (links, buttons, summaries, fields, labels and the button, tab, option, menuitem and switch roles) a transition on its colours, opacity, shadow and transforms, at the motion duration and easing; reduced motion cuts it. The examples lint requires a transition class on any other element that changes with state.

  • Indicators follow state

    New rotate-0/45/90/180 and -rotate-45/90/180; group-open: now also matches a group holding an open popover and takes rotations, so a caret with transition group-open:rotate-180 turns over when its disclosure or menu opens.

  • Glass lens, after Apple's Liquid Glass

    lens refracts what is behind a glass part. The site's script draws a displacement map for each lens at its own size and corner radius: the surface refracts like a rounded glass edge (the bend follows the slope of a quarter-circle bevel, steep at the rim and easing across a band up to a third of the part's height), the whole body magnifies slightly, and red, green and blue bend by slightly different amounts. Clear glass drops its gloss band and blur (0.5px) and catches the light as two thin crescents on opposite corners. The lens runs where the page sets data-lens="on", which the script does in Chromium, the only engine that applies an SVG filter in backdrop-filter; elsewhere glass keeps its blur. The showcase has a glass slider over a track to show it.

  • Light and dark toggle

    A button in the band switches the scheme and remembers it; a head script applies the choice before the page paints.

  • Dot grid

    bg-dots paints a dot grid in currentColor, spaced by bg-dots-sm, -md or -lg. The materials demo uses it in place of coloured stripes, so the blur of frosted and glass is easy to read.

  • Examples in primitives

    Every component snippet and showcase block now uses regular classes only (palette steps, the space scale, type sizes), checked by scripts/templates.py --lint. The materials, shell and tiers demos keep their semantic classes. To keep hover and states, the raw steps of neutral, accent-1 to accent-4 and danger now take hover and state variants (nowind.css 236 → 381 KiB, the minified bundle 41 → 52.5 KB gzipped), and new fg-{palette}-contrast classes give text on a solid.

  • Breaking, state variants without `aria-`

    aria-selected: is now selected:, aria-current: is current:, aria-pressed: is pressed:, aria-expanded: is expanded: and aria-invalid: is invalid:. They still match the same ARIA attributes.

  • Field slots

    A field is a box around a bare input with five slots: leading icon, prefix, input, suffix, trailing. New state variants focus-within: and has-invalid: let the box show the input's focus and errors. An h-nested or size-nested control at either end of a control now takes the same inset as an icon.

  • Text field docs

    Slots, ten data types (password with a show toggle, card number, date, time, weight, percentage, quantity, colour, postal code, duration) with the right type, inputmode and autocomplete, and the invalid, counted, read-only and disabled states.

  • One control recipe

    Buttons, links, inputs, selects and textareas share height, side padding, a border width (transparent on fills, so a solid button and a field line up to the pixel), radius and type. New rounded-control and rounded-nested. scripts/check.mjs flags a control that drifts.

  • One caret

    caret on an icon box draws the disclosure chevron in CSS, and select-base draws the same one as its picker icon. Both turn over when open ([open], aria-expanded="true", an open select).

  • Native controls

    The reset themes the file input's button, progress and meter (which turns success, warning or danger from its low, high and optimum), with system colours in forced colours. New field-sizing-content and field-sizing-fixed.

  • Select

    select-base centres the chosen value in the field, the highlighted option shows by its background with no focus ring, and the picker arrow takes the weak text colour.

  • Focus

    Text fields (text-like inputs, textarea, select) no longer get the focus ring; their border turns accent instead. Buttons, links and checkable controls keep the ring, and in forced colours a focused field gets a Highlight outline.

  • One page template

    Every page (docs, component pages, the showcase, the specimen, the utilities demo, the landing page) shares one frame after Vignelli's Unigrid: an inverse band with the four sections, a bar with a site-map menu (every section and page, no script), a search field and previous and next, and blocks with their label in three of twelve columns. The docs sidebar, phone menu and on-page contents are gone.

  • Search

    Press ⌘K, Ctrl+K or / on any page to search every page, block heading, component and class. site/search.json is written with the pages.

  • Class lists

    Roles named after accent-1 to accent-4 (bg-accent-1, bg-component-accent-4) now list as semantic, not regular.

  • Docs frame

    The page scrolls as a whole. The header is sticky, the sidebar sticks under it, and main is no longer a scroll box. Below 1024px only the Menu row shows, the GitHub button is icon-only below 1280px, tables wrap instead of scrolling sideways, and the class lists become two-line rows on a phone. New scroll-pt-header and scroll-mt-* keep linked rows clear of sticky bars.

  • Breaking, background and border role classes

    The role group is dropped when it matches the prefix, as fg- already did: bg-bg is bg, bg-bg-accent-1 is bg-accent-1, border-border-weak is border-weak. Plain border-border is border-default, and the reset now draws every border in --border, so border alone needs no colour class.

  • Breaking, text colour classes

    Raw steps and on-solid now take fg- like the text roles: text-accent-1-11 is fg-accent-1-11, text-on-solid-danger is fg-on-solid-danger. text-inherit and text-current are fg-inherit and fg-current. Every text colour class now starts with fg.

  • Site moved

    The landing page, showcase, specimen and utilities demo are in site/, the docs in site/docs/. The root index.html redirects to site/.

  • Classes page

    site/docs/classes.html lists all 1349 base classes by category with their declarations, a preview for colours, borders, radius and shadow, and a filter, under a grid linking to each group. Regular classes (a scale step or a value: p-md, border-warning-7) and semantic classes (a role: p-surface, fg-weak, text-heading-md) get separate pages, classes.html and semantic.html. Component pages link each class to its row.

0.1.0

2026-11-25

First release.

Theme

  • Colors

    Nine palettes on the radix-hct-colors scales: accent-1 (blue), accent-2 (violet), accent-3 (plum), accent-4 (crimson), neutral (slate), danger (red), warning (amber), success (green), info (indigo). Each has steps 1–12, alpha steps a1–a12 and a contrast color, all in light-dark().

  • Color roles

    bg, component, border, solid, on-solid and fg for every palette, with -weak and -strong steps; Radix companions surface, indicator, track; --scrim for backdrops.

  • Type

    A fluid Utopia scale --text-xs … --text-6xl with Tailwind v4 line-height companions; 29 semantic roles after Massimo (--heading-md, --body-md, --figure, …); a kicker at three sizes.

  • Geometry

    Every length from one base size --F, after Affine UI: slot, margin, inset, control height, radii, icon sizes, container levels and tiers. At the root --F follows the fluid body size; a .scope or tier sets its own.

  • Space

    --space-3xs … --space-4xl as multiples of --F.

  • Shadows

    Compound casts and recesses after Massimo, --shadow-xs … --shadow-xl and --inset-shadow-xs, --inset-shadow-sm.

Utilities

  • About 1,200 Prewind-style classes with Tailwind v4 names where they overlap; hover: for role colors, opacity and cursor; sm: … 2xl: for layout.

  • Affine container levels (p-surface, p-dialog, gap-field, gap-group, …), tiers (tier-content, tier-brand), control and icon utilities for Phosphor icons.

  • A 2026 reset in @layer reset, every selector in :where().

Tooling

  • dist/nowind.min.css: the whole system in one file.

  • Generators for the type and space scales, shadows, utilities and bundle; scripts/check.mjs with a self-test.

  • specimen.html, components.html, utilities.html and classes.md.
