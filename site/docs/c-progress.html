Progress bar

Native progress and meter, themed by the reset, or a track and a fill sized with a fraction class.

Example

64%
64%
9.1 of 10 GB
9.1 of 10 GB
Storage6.4 of 10 GB

Usage

Use
  • Always print the value next to the bar.
  • progress for a task under way; meter for a level within a range, which turns warning or danger past its high and low.
Avoid
  • A bar for something that isn't a share of a whole.
