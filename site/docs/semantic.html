Semantic classes

Semantic classes name a role in the design rather than a value: a container level (p-surface, gap-field), a colour role (fg-weak, bg-solid-accent-1), a type role (text-heading-md), a control length, a material or a tier. Their values follow the theme. 255 of them, with what each declares, read from classes.md. The rest are in Regular classes. Colour, opacity and cursor classes also take hover: and the state variants; layout classes take sm: to 2xl:.
