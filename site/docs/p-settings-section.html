Settings section

A titled group of settings, one per row: what it does on the left, its control on the right, a rule between rows. On a phone each row stacks.

Example

Notifications

Choose what reaches your inbox.
MentionsWhen someone mentions you in a comment.
Weekly summaryA digest of the week's changes, on Monday mornings.
LanguageUsed for emails and dates.

Usage

Use
  • Name each control after its row (aria-label), since the row's text is not its label.
  • Switches take effect at once; keep Save for settings that wait for it.
Avoid
  • A switch and a Save button for the same setting.
