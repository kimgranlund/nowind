Materials

A material is how a region is painted: flat, lit, frosted, clear glass or outlined. It never changes a length, so switching material moves nothing. Set it once on a region; every part inside paints itself by what it is.

Materials

ClassWhat it does
material-flatNo paint: the default. Overlays still cast.
material-physicalLit: face shading, a bevel (lit top, shaded bottom, half-pixel ring) and the raised cast; pressed sinks to a recess; fields and wells are recessed; cards cast at the popup level.
material-frostedLit, and raised parts, cards and overlays are frosted glass: a 56% face over an 8px blur, a specular sheen, a lit rim and the cast.
material-glassLit, and raised parts, cards and overlays are clear glass after Apple's Liquid Glass: an 18% face over a 0.5px blur, no gloss band, and the light catching the rim as two thin crescents on opposite corners. Add lens for refraction.
material-outlineNo fills but state: every part draws one 1px line in the muted text color; overlays stay opaque.

Parts

Set a material on a region with material-physical, material-frosted, material-glass or material-outline (material-flat resets it). Parts say what they are with relief-raised (buttons), relief-raised-solid (primary buttons), relief-recessed (tracks, wells), relief-field (inputs), relief-etched, relief-ground (cards) and relief-overlay (popovers, dialogs). The nearest material wins: the classes are @scope rules that stop at a nested material.

Glass and the lens

Lit, and raised parts, cards and overlays are clear glass after Apple's Liquid Glass: an 18% face over a 0.5px blur, no gloss band, and the light catching the rim as two thin crescents on opposite corners. Add lens for refraction.

The lens class refracts the backdrop of a glass part through an SVG displacement filter: site/site.js draws a map per element at its size and corner radius (a rounded-edge bend along the rim, a slight overall magnification, an R/G/B fringe) and sets data-lens="on" on the root in Chromium, the only engine that applies an SVG filter in backdrop-filter. Elsewhere glass keeps its blur. A page outside this site supplies the filter itself.

Glass slider: drag itLens
Note

Only Chromium applies an SVG filter behind an element. Elsewhere glass keeps its blur, so text over it must read without the lens.

Backdrops

Glass needs something behind it to bend. bg-dots paints a dot grid in currentColor, spaced by bg-dots-xs, -sm, -md or -lg: the showcase sets its materials on one.
