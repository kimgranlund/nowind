# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Callouts in primitives:** the docs' callouts and the callout sample are `p-md border-s-4 border-{palette}-7 bg-{palette}-3` with `fg-{palette}-11` icons, `text-sm font-medium fg-{palette}-12` labels and `text-base` text, in place of role classes; the block sample's heading is `text-heading-md`, as the pages use.
- **One corner for alerts:** one-line alerts are `rounded-md`, like titled alerts and toasts.
- **Pressed segments are white in both modes, flat:** `pressed:bg-white pressed:fg-black` with no shadow. New `bg-white`, `bg-black`, `fg-white` and `fg-black` (the neutral contrast ends, the same in light and dark), with state variants.
- **Unread dot:** a `block` span, so its padding draws a circle; inline, it took the line's height and drew a tall pill.
- **Tag chips fixed:** a chip is `px-nested` with no fixed height, so its `size-nested` remove button sets the height and `px-nested` drops to zero at that trailing control: the circle meets the pill on three sides; the badge pass had stacked `px-nested` on the chip's own padding. The examples lint now catches a shorthand padding (`p-`, `px-`, `py-`) set alongside a one-side padding.
- **Soft buttons without a border:** the accent soft button is `bg-accent-1-3` with a transparent border, matching the secondary button.
- **No clipped cards when rows stack:** items that share a row use `md:flex-1`, not `flex-1`, so below `md`, where the row becomes a column, they keep their content height; the media card had shrunk to zero basis and `overflow-hidden` cut off its text.
- **Menus anchor to their trigger:** the select's picker and every `anchor-*` popover start flush with the trigger's edge and are at least as wide as it (`anchor-size(width)`), with the 3xs gap only on the side facing it; before, the gap applied on every side and the picker floated inset and narrower than its field.
- **Lighter field borders:** text fields, selects and textareas keep their white face and a visible border, lighter now: `border-neutral-5` at rest, `hover:border-neutral-7`, accent on focus (was 6 and 8); the reset gives a bare field `--border-weak`.
- **Nested parts, one geometry:** badges, the actions inside alerts and toasts, and toolbar items are all the small control: `h-nested` with `px-nested` (which steps in at an icon), and the alert's secondary action is a soft tint (`bg-danger-a3`) instead of bare text. A count badge and the stepper's numbers are 1:1 circles (`size-nested`, `text-xs`), with a count row in the size contract. The toolbar and segmented containers take the secondary buttons' light tint (`bg-neutral-a2`).
- **Badges share one geometry:** every badge is `h-nested`, `rounded-full` and bordered (transparent on solid fills), padded `px-2xs`, or `ps-3xs pe-2xs` with a leading icon; a count is `min-w-nested` so it stays round. Outlined and solid badges were different heights, and the Beta tag a different shape.
- **Secondary buttons, softer:** a light translucent neutral tint (`bg-neutral-a2`) with no visible border (`border border-transparent`, so the height is unchanged) in place of a grey fill and a mid-grey border; the state layer gives hover and press. The file input's button matches.
- **Site map links work on the showcase:** the showcase's demo script, which moves `aria-current` on click, caught every link in a `role="list"` list, the site map's included, and cancelled the navigation. It now acts only on placeholder links (`href="#"`) inside the page's main content.
- **Site map links:** each link is as wide as its label with the nested corner (`w-fit rounded-xs`), so a hover is a small wash rather than a full-column pill, and the current page is marked with an accent tint instead of the same grey as a hover.
## 0.9.0 (2026-11-25)

- **Docs chrome on the size contract:** the Use and Avoid marks are `icon` beside body text (they were `icon-sm`), and a code caption sets its language label at the filename's size.
- **Leaner variants (60.4 → 47.2 KiB gzip for the linked file):** hover and state variants cover the colour roles plus the raw steps a control moves between (surfaces 1–5, solids 9–10, text 11–12 and contrast, borders 6–8) in neutral, accent-1 and danger; other steps use `state`. Breakpoints cover display, grid, flex, alignment, widths, inline padding and gaps (no position, heights or overflow). Container queries are `@sm:`, `@md:`, `@lg:` over the classes that change a component's layout. Nothing the site uses was removed.
- **Stronger hovers:** the state layer's opacities are 12% hover, 14% focus, 16% selected and 20% pressed (from 8, 10, 12 and 16), and the site map's links take the state layer with a nested-control shape.
- **Section titles:** the site's section headings (showcase, utilities and every docs page) are `text-heading-md` (the `text-xl` step) instead of `text-subtitle` (the base size), so they stand above the body text under them.
- **Tailwind's scales, then Nowind's systems:** every type step is Tailwind v4's fraction of the base (xs 0.75 … 6xl 3.75) with Tailwind's line heights, so at a 16px base the sizes are Tailwind's exactly; Utopia's fluid base, the root size, tiers and `--F` then act on that base. The Utopia ratio options (`--min-ratio`, `--max-ratio`) are gone. Space, breakpoints and container widths already matched Tailwind's. Large steps grow in proportion to the base now, rather than faster than it on wide screens.
- **A gentler small step:** `text-sm` is 0.875 × base and `text-xs` 0.75 × base (14 and 12px at 16, Tailwind's ratios) at every width, instead of one and two Utopia ratio steps down (13.3 and 11px), which made supporting text drop too far below body text. The steps above base keep Utopia's ratio; `icon-sm` follows text-sm (0.875 F).
- **Select picker in Phosphor, nested:** the select's caret, the `caret` class and the selected option's checkmark are Phosphor's caret-down and check (bold), inlined as mask tokens (`--glyph-caret`, `--glyph-check`), so nothing extra loads. The picker is `rounded-md` padded by the inset and its options are a control tall, `rounded-sm`, so their corners nest concentrically. 
- **Breadcrumbs at the navigation size:** `text-base`, like the tabs and links around them; the size contract gains a navigation row.
- **Sizes examples:** the Button, Icon button, Segmented control and Text field pages show small, default and large. A size is a region with its own base: `scope text-sm` or `scope text-lg` around the component, so height, padding, gaps, icons and corners all recompute and still nest (25, 37 and 51px at a 16px root), and the component's text inherits the scope's size. Both type audits treat a scope as relative.
- **Softer curvature:** `--radius-offset` is -4px (Affine uses -6px), so every radius step gains 2px: at 16px the control corner is 12px and the nested step 5px, where 3px had read as sharp on keycaps and nested items. Nesting stays exact. Keycaps set `text-sm`.
- **README rewritten on Prewind's structure:** what Nowind is for (a runtime, no-dependency foundation for agents and generated UI, with sensible defaults and a retunable token layer), what it derives from (Prewind, Tailwind's class system, Radix Colors, Utopia) and what it adds (one-input geometry, root-scaled fluid sizing, tiers); a Nowind, Prewind and Tailwind comparison, a reference and a variants table. Stale examples (a padded field box, semantic classes) replaced with the current patterns.
- **Type audit:** a size contract in the type-roles manifest (`sizeContract`) says which size each kind of text takes; `python3 scripts/templates.py --type-report` checks every example element against it, and `?audit=type` on any page checks the rendered examples for rows that mix sizes, titles smaller than their body, and icons a step off their text. The sweep brought every example onto the contract: 12 body paragraphs, 8 pricing features (with their ✓ at `icon`), 6 activity and notification lines (inline names and code now take the line's size) and the drop zone title move to `text-base`; the spinner beside small text is `icon-sm` and the selected-option check `icon`. Both passes now report nothing, and the examples lint enforces the contract (self-test: sixteen breaks); the Type and space page has a "Where each size goes" table.
- **`icon-sm` is the small text step:** `--icon-sm` is F ÷ 1.2 (13.3px at 16) instead of F, so a small icon matches the `text-sm` label beside it in chips, badges and toolbars, where it had been larger than the text; carets get finer too. Marks keep their size (`--mark-sm` is F).
- **Dialog type:** the title is `text-lg`, a step above the controls, and the body `text-base`, the controls' size (it had been `text-base` and `text-sm`, so the body read smaller than the buttons and the title no larger); footer buttons sit `gap-xs` apart.
- **Actions in toasts and alerts nest:** Retry, Undo, View logs and Dismiss take `rounded-inner`, the surface's radius less its padding, instead of `rounded-xs`, the radius for an item one inset inside a control, which read as square at a toast's padding. The titled alert pads `p-xs`, as the toast does (at `p-sm` the inner radius came to 1.5px).
- **Dialog example:** the close button is a fixed `size-nested shrink-0` square again (an earlier toolbar change had made it `h-full aspect-square`, so it grew with a wrapped title), and the dialog example has its own full row (capped at `max-w-form`), so its title and its Cancel and Delete buttons no longer wrap; the buttons sit `gap-xs` apart.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-25)

First release.

### Theme

- **Colors:** nine palettes on the radix-hct-colors scales: `accent-1` (blue), `accent-2` (violet), `accent-3` (plum), `accent-4` (crimson), `neutral` (slate), `danger` (red), `warning` (amber), `success` (green), `info` (indigo). Each has steps 1–12, alpha steps a1–a12 and a contrast color, all in `light-dark()`.
- **Color roles:** `bg`, `component`, `border`, `solid`, `on-solid` and `fg` for every palette, with `-weak` and `-strong` steps; Radix companions `surface`, `indicator`, `track`; `--scrim` for backdrops.
- **Type:** a fluid Utopia scale `--text-xs` … `--text-6xl` with Tailwind v4 line-height companions; 29 semantic roles after Massimo (`--heading-md`, `--body-md`, `--figure`, …); a kicker at three sizes.
- **Geometry:** every length from one base size `--F`, after Affine UI: slot, margin, inset, control height, radii, icon sizes, container levels and tiers. At the root `--F` follows the fluid body size; a `.scope` or tier sets its own.
- **Space:** `--space-3xs` … `--space-4xl` as multiples of `--F`.
- **Shadows:** compound casts and recesses after Massimo, `--shadow-xs` … `--shadow-xl` and `--inset-shadow-xs`, `--inset-shadow-sm`.

### Utilities

- About 1,200 Prewind-style classes with Tailwind v4 names where they overlap; `hover:` for role colors, opacity and cursor; `sm:` … `2xl:` for layout.
- Affine container levels (`p-surface`, `p-dialog`, `gap-field`, `gap-group`, …), tiers (`tier-content`, `tier-brand`), control and icon utilities for Phosphor icons.
- A 2026 reset in `@layer reset`, every selector in `:where()`.

### Tooling

- `dist/nowind.min.css`: the whole system in one file.
- Generators for the type and space scales, shadows, utilities and bundle; `scripts/check.mjs` with a self-test.
- `specimen.html`, `components.html`, `utilities.html` and `classes.md`.
